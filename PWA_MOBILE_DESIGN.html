How to Install

1

On Android (Chrome)

Open the website, tap the menu (3 dots), select "Add to Home screen" or "Install app". The app will appear on your home screen.

2

On iPhone (Safari)

Open the website, tap the Share button, scroll down and select "Add to Home Screen". The app icon will be added to your home screen.

3

On Desktop (Chrome/Edge)

Look for the install icon in the address bar, or go to Settings > Apps > Install this site as an app. The app will open in its own window.

4

Enjoy the App

Once installed, you can launch PreEvent like any other app. It will work offline, send notifications, and provide a native app experience!
