How do I book a service?
To book a service, browse our categories, select the service you need, choose your preferred date and time slot, add it to cart, and proceed to checkout. You can pay via cash on booking or online payment.
Can I cancel my booking?
Yes, you can cancel your booking if it's still pending. Go to "My Orders" and click on "Cancel Order". For accepted orders, please contact our support team.
What payment methods are accepted?
We accept cash on booking and online payments through various payment gateways. Online payment options include credit/debit cards, UPI, net banking, and digital wallets.
