# PreEvent Marketing Video Creation Guide

## 🎬 Video Content Ideas

### 1. **Platform Introduction Video (60-90 seconds)**
**Purpose:** Explain what PreEvent is and how it works

**Script Outline:**
- **Hook (0-5s):** "Planning an event? We've got you covered!"
- **Problem (5-15s):** Show the stress of event planning - multiple vendors, coordination issues
- **Solution (15-45s):** Introduce PreEvent as the one-stop platform
  - Browse services (weddings, corporate, birthdays)
  - Book photographers, makeup artists, caterers
  - Easy booking process
- **Benefits (45-75s):** 
  - Save time
  - Trusted vendors
  - Best prices
  - Wide assortment
- **Call to Action (75-90s):** "Visit preevent.in and make your event unforgettable!"

---

### 2. **Feature Highlight Video (30-45 seconds)**
**Purpose:** Showcase key features quickly

**Content:**
- Wide assortment of services
- Best prices and offers
- Easy booking process
- Trusted vendors
- Mobile-friendly platform

---

### 3. **Customer Success Story (60-90 seconds)**
**Purpose:** Build trust through real experiences

**Structure:**
- Customer testimonial (real or scripted)
- Showcase their event
- Highlight services used from PreEvent
- Show happy moments

---

### 4. **Service-Specific Videos (30-45 seconds each)**
Create separate short videos for:
- Wedding Planning
- Corporate Events
- Photography Services
- Makeup Artists
- Catering Services

---

## 📝 Step-by-Step Video Creation Process

### **Step 1: Choose Your Video Type**
Start with the **Platform Introduction Video** - it's the most important for new visitors.

### **Step 2: Write Your Script**
Use the script outline above and customize it with:
- Your unique selling points
- Specific features you want to highlight
- Your brand voice and tone

### **Step 3: Gather Visual Assets**
You'll need:
- **Screenshots/Screen Recordings** of your website
- **Stock Photos/Videos** of events (weddings, corporate events, etc.)
- **Logo and Brand Colors**
- **Product Images** from your website

**Where to get free assets:**
- Unsplash.com (free photos)
- Pexels.com (free photos & videos)
- Pixabay.com (free photos & videos)

### **Step 4: Choose Your Video Creation Tool**

#### **Option A: Free/Simple Tools (Beginner-Friendly)**
1. **Canva** (canva.com)
   - Free video templates
   - Easy drag-and-drop
   - Built-in stock media
   - Export as MP4

2. **InVideo** (invideo.io)
   - Free plan available
   - Templates for marketing videos
   - Text-to-video AI features

3. **CapCut** (capcut.com)
   - Free video editor
   - Mobile app available
   - Easy to use

#### **Option B: Professional Tools**
1. **Adobe Premiere Pro** (paid)
2. **Final Cut Pro** (Mac only, paid)
3. **DaVinci Resolve** (free, professional)

#### **Option C: AI-Powered Tools (Easiest)**
1. **Synthesia** (synthesia.io) - AI avatars
2. **Pictory** (pictory.ai) - Text to video
3. **Loom** (loom.com) - Screen recording with narration

### **Step 5: Create Your Video**

**For Beginners - Using Canva:**
1. Go to canva.com and create account
2. Search "marketing video" templates
3. Choose a template (60-90 seconds)
4. Replace text with your script
5. Add your website screenshots
6. Add stock event photos/videos
7. Add your logo
8. Choose background music (Canva has free music)
9. Export as MP4 (1080p recommended)

**Video Specifications:**
- **Duration:** 60-90 seconds (optimal for social media)
- **Aspect Ratio:** 
  - 16:9 for YouTube, website
  - 9:16 for Instagram Reels, TikTok
  - 1:1 for Instagram posts
- **Resolution:** 1080p (1920x1080) minimum
- **Format:** MP4

### **Step 6: Add Voiceover/Narration**

**Options:**
1. **Record yourself** using phone or microphone
2. **Use AI voiceover:**
   - ElevenLabs (elevenlabs.io)
   - Murf (murf.ai)
   - Canva's built-in text-to-speech
3. **Hire a voiceover artist** on Fiverr (affordable)

**Tips:**
- Speak clearly and enthusiastically
- Match the pace to your visuals
- Add background music (keep it subtle)

### **Step 7: Add Background Music**

**Free Music Sources:**
- YouTube Audio Library
- Free Music Archive
- Canva's music library
- Pixabay Music

**Choose music that:**
- Matches your brand (upbeat, professional, elegant)
- Doesn't overpower the voiceover
- Is royalty-free

### **Step 8: Finalize and Export**

**Before exporting, check:**
- ✅ All text is readable
- ✅ Logo is visible
- ✅ Website URL is shown clearly
- ✅ Call to action is prominent
- ✅ Video flows smoothly
- ✅ Audio is clear
