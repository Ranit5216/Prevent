# PreEvent - 30 Second Promotional Video Script

## 🎬 Video Overview
**Duration:** 30 seconds  
**Purpose:** Brand awareness and user acquisition  
**Target Audience:** People planning events (weddings, corporate, birthdays)  
**Tone:** Energetic, trustworthy, solution-focused

---

## 📝 Complete Script with Timing

### **SCENE 1: Hook (0-3 seconds)**
**Visual:** Quick montage of beautiful event moments (wedding, party, corporate event)
**Text Overlay:** "Planning an event?"
**Voiceover:** "Planning an event?"

---

### **SCENE 2: Problem (3-6 seconds)**
**Visual:** Person looking stressed, multiple vendor contacts on phone, calendar chaos
**Text Overlay:** "Too many vendors to coordinate?"
**Voiceover:** "Too many vendors to coordinate?"

---

### **SCENE 3: Solution Introduction (6-12 seconds)**
**Visual:** PreEvent logo appears, website interface showing categories
**Text Overlay:** "PreEvent - Your One-Stop Event Planning Platform"
**Voiceover:** "Meet PreEvent - India's premier event planning platform. One place for all your event needs."

---

### **SCENE 4: Services Showcase (12-20 seconds)**
**Visual:** Quick cuts showing:
- Wedding photography
- Makeup artist at work
- Catering setup
- Decoration
- Corporate event
**Text Overlay:** "Photography | Makeup | Catering | Decoration | & More"
**Voiceover:** "Photographers, makeup artists, caterers, decorators - we have 500+ trusted vendors across 50+ categories."

---

### **SCENE 5: Benefits (20-25 seconds)**
**Visual:** Website interface showing easy booking, ratings, prices
**Text Overlay:** "Best Prices | Trusted Vendors | Easy Booking"
**Voiceover:** "Best prices, verified vendors, easy booking - all in one place."

---

### **SCENE 6: Call to Action (25-30 seconds)**
**Visual:** PreEvent logo with website URL
**Text Overlay:** "Visit preevent.in | Start Planning Today!"
**Voiceover:** "Visit preevent.in and make your event unforgettable. Start planning today!"

---

## 🎨 Visual Style Guide

### **Color Palette:**
- **Primary:** Red (#DC2626) - Brand color
- **Secondary:** White, Light Gray backgrounds
- **Accents:** Gold for highlights

### **Typography:**
- **Headings:** Bold, modern sans-serif
- **Body:** Clean, readable font
- **Logo:** PreEvent brand font

### **Visual Elements:**
- Fast-paced cuts (2-3 seconds per scene)
- Smooth transitions
- High-quality event footage
- Website screenshots/animations
- Happy faces, celebrations
- Professional vendor shots

---

## 🎵 Music & Sound

### **Music Style:**
- Upbeat, energetic background music
- Starts soft, builds to energetic peak
- Modern, professional tone
- No lyrics (instrumental)

### **Sound Effects:**
- Subtle whoosh for transitions
- Click sound for website interactions
- Celebration sounds (subtle)

## 🚀 Post-Production Tips

1. **Color Grading:** Warm, inviting tones
2. **Pacing:** Fast but not overwhelming
3. **Text Animation:** Smooth fade-ins, slide effects
4. **Logo Placement:** Consistent position (top-right or center)
5. **CTA:** Make website URL prominent and readable
6. **End Screen:** Logo + URL visible for 2-3 seconds

---

## 📊 Success Metrics

Track these after video launch:
- Views and engagement rate
- Click-through rate to website
- Brand awareness surveys
- Registration conversions from video traffic

---

## 🎬 Final Notes

- Keep it simple and clear
- Focus on benefits, not features
- Show real emotions (happiness, celebration)
- Make the CTA impossible to miss
- Test on mobile devices (most viewers will watch on phone)
